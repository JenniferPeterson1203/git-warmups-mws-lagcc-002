## Questions to answer in your daily log

- What caused the conflict, in your own words?
The conflict was caused because I was making changes in the same file and on the same line on 2 different branches so when it was time to merge the two files, it was unclear which to keep
- What do the `<<<`, `===`, `>>>` markers mean?
 <<< = the current change on the branch that you are currently on
>>> == the incoming change from the other branch you were working on
- How did you decide what to keep?
I decided to just keep them both 
- Was this scary or fine? Honest answer.
This is always scary to me when I initally see some form of error but I try to remind myself that errors are not always bad and they can be helpful in regards to helping you to solve the conflicts.

## The rule about merge conflicts
They are normal. Professional developers resolve them weekly. The only thing that makes them scary is thinking they shouldn't happen.
